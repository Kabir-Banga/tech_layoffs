# 📊 Tech Layoffs, Hiring Trends & AI Impact Dashboard

A comprehensive Power BI dashboard analyzing the relationship between layoffs, hiring demand, AI adoption, employee sentiment, remote work trends, and market conditions across major technology companies and industries.

## 🚀 Project Overview

News headlines often focus on layoffs, creating the perception that opportunities in tech are disappearing. However, the data tells a much more nuanced story.

This dashboard explores whether layoffs truly indicate a shrinking job market or if companies are simultaneously creating new opportunities while restructuring their workforce.

Using interactive visualizations, this project uncovers trends in hiring demand, AI adoption, employee sentiment, revenue growth, and workforce transformation.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations worldwide are adopting AI, restructuring teams, and optimizing costs, resulting in significant layoffs across industries.

At the same time, millions of job openings continue to exist.

This project aims to answer:

- Are layoffs reducing overall employment opportunities?
- Which industries are most affected by workforce reductions?
- How is AI influencing hiring and replacement risk?
- Which job roles remain in high demand?
- How do hiring trends affect employee sentiment?

---

## 📌 Key Insights

### 1️⃣ Layoffs Do Not Mean Hiring Has Stopped

One of the most surprising findings was that despite more than **60 million layoffs**, companies still reported over **34 million open roles**.

This suggests that the market is experiencing a skills transition rather than a complete decline in opportunities.

### 2️⃣ AI Is Creating Both Disruption and Demand

Industries with high AI adoption levels also showed some of the strongest hiring demand.

Rather than eliminating jobs entirely, AI appears to be reshaping workforce requirements and creating demand for new skill sets.

### 3️⃣ Employee Sentiment Reflects Hiring Confidence

Companies experiencing aggressive hiring demonstrated higher employee sentiment scores, while organizations undergoing downsizing showed lower morale.

### 4️⃣ AI, Cloud & Cybersecurity Lead Future Demand

These industries consistently ranked among the highest for both hiring activity and business growth, highlighting where future opportunities may exist.
